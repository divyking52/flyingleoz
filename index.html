[ The studio ]

The functions behind the work

Four seats carry a project from brief to distribution — planning, production, design and social.

Strategy & Consulting

Planning, positioning, measurement

Turns the business goal into a brief: audience, message, channel mix and the numbers a campaign has to move.

Film & Production

Direction, camera, sound, edit

Runs the shoot and the post after it, from brand films and interviews down to the cut-downs each platform needs.

Design & Content

Key visuals, statics, motion

Builds the look of a campaign and keeps it consistent across posters, carousels, thumbnails and packaging artwork.

Social & Creator Ops

Calendars, shoots, community

Plans the monthly calendar, runs the creator shoots and keeps the inboxes, comments and posting schedule moving.

What does Flying Leoz Media do?

Comprehensive media and marketing solutions tailored for your business growth. The six services published on the current site are Business Consultancy, Marketing & Strategy, Content Creation, Media Strategy, Marketing Support and Digital Marketing Services.

Who do you work with?

Startups and established businesses. The published brief is to help both “grow with tailored strategies” — and to help them grow with confidence.

What does “full service” mean here?

“In India, the concept of a full-service agency is not very well-known, and most of the time, all we can explain is that we do our magic and show you the results!”

How do you approach a campaign?

By crafting targeted campaigns that connect your brand with the right audience effectively. You see the outcome as the work itself, presented back to you.

Where are you based?

Flying Leoz Media Pvt. Ltd. — B-114, Block A, Sector-64, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201301. Studio hours listed on the current site are 10am–7pm.

What does a project cost?

Three ways to engage are listed under Packages: Launch Sprint at ₹35,000 per project, Always-On at ₹85,000 per month and Full Service on a quoted retainer. Work that fits none of the three is quoted to the brief.

How long does a project take?

A single brand film takes about ten working days from a signed brief to final cut, and includes two revision rounds. Retainer work follows the monthly content calendar agreed at kickoff, so the first assets are delivered inside the first week.
